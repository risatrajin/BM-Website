AI Workflow · Under the hood

What happens to a client conversation — step by step.

What goes in, what the AI does with it, and what comes out ready for your approval.

Goes in

What MIA works from

  • Meeting audioVideo call or in person — with consent
  • Documents & IDsPolicies, statements, ID scans
  • Existing client dataPast notes, family, holdings
  • EmailsClient replies and attachments

The AI Workflow

What MIA does with it

  1. 1
    Listen & transcribeTurns the conversation into an accurate, speaker-labelled transcript.
  2. 2
    Understand & extractPicks out goals, budget, dependents, risks and products discussed.
  3. 3
    Check against the rulesTests KYC and suitability for completeness against regulator requirements.
  4. 4
    Draft the workPrepares notes, forms, rationale and follow-ups — never sends them.
  5. 5
    You approveAdvisor reviews, edits and signs off in one screen.
  6. 6
    Record everythingTime-stamps each step into the audit trail, visible in Harmony.

Comes out

Ready for your approval

  • Meeting summary & next steps
  • KYC & FNA fields filled
  • Suitability rationale
  • E-sign package
  • Follow-up email draft
  • Audit trail entry
