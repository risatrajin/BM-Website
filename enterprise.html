Everything Harmony does

Six capabilities. One command centre.

What your compliance team gets on day one.

  1. Supervision without the friction.

    Harmony sits on top of your existing agency back office and watches advisor activity in real time — advisors keep working the way they do.

    • No change to advisors’ daily toolsAdvisors keep their CRM, email and calendar. Harmony reads in the background.
    • Live feed of meetings, KYC and salesActivity arrives as it happens, not at month end.
    • Works with Advisor and your back officeConnects to your agency systems, CRMs and carrier feeds.

  2. See the problem before the auditor does.

    BlueMind’s predictive model spots behavioural anomalies and missing records and flags them with a clear reason.

    • Unsuitable replacements and missing notesFlagged the day they happen, with the file attached.
    • Lapse spikes and unusual patternsSpot outliers by advisor, agency or product.
    • Every flag explains why it firedA plain-language reason, so reviewers know where to look.

  3. Your whole network on one screen.

    KYC, suitability, E&O and licensing status for every advisor — filterable by region, team or product.

    • Network compliance score and trendOne score for the whole network, tracked week over week.
    • Drill down from network to advisor to clientGo from the big picture to a single file without switching tools.
    • Saved views for each teamEach team opens straight to the advisors and risks it owns.

  4. No more expired certificates.

    Track licences, appointments and E&O certificates for every advisor, with automatic reminders before anything lapses.

    • Expiry tracking with automatic remindersAdvisors are reminded before a licence or E&O certificate lapses.
    • Certificate upload and verificationAdvisors upload, your team verifies, and the record is kept.
    • Status visible on every advisor profileLicence and E&O status sit on each advisor’s profile.

  5. From flag to fixed — on record.

    Assign reviews, request documents, add notes and close flags in one place. Every action is logged.

    • Assign to reviewers with due datesEvery flag has an owner and a deadline.
    • Request documents from advisors in-appAsk for the missing document without leaving Harmony.
    • Resolution notes saved to the audit trailEvery decision is time-stamped and kept on record.

  6. Proof in minutes, not weeks.

    Export a complete, time-stamped evidence file for any advisor, client, product or period.

    • One-click export (PDF)A complete evidence file for any advisor, client or period.
    • Proof-of-work trail behind every itemEach line links to the review, note or document behind it.
    • Scheduled reports for leadershipMonthly summaries reach leadership automatically.
