# French (fr-CA) glossary for the BlueMind site

Audience: financial and insurance advisors, agencies and carriers in Canada (mostly Quebec and Ontario).
Write natural Canadian French (OQLF-friendly), the way a Quebec fintech would market itself. Use "vous".

## Keep in English (proper names)
BlueMind, Mia, BlueMind Advisor / Advisor (product), BlueMind Enterprise / Enterprise (product),
FSRA, SEC, FINRA, MGA, BGA, IMO, HubSpot, Google, Gmail, Google Calendar, LinkedIn, CFP, people's names.
Nav labels "Advisor", "Enterprise", "Mia" stay as-is (product names).

## Typography
- Typographic apostrophe ’ (never ').
- Non-breaking space (U+00A0) before : ; ? ! and inside « » quotes.
- Money: "79 $" / "169 $ CA" (amount, NBSP, $). Thousands: "12 000".
- Times: "10 h", "7 h 08", "15 h".
- Dates: "12 juin", "12 mars".
- Sentence case for headings, like the English.
