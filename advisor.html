Everything Advisor does

Six features. One AI workflow.

Each one removes a job you used to do by hand.

  1. Talk to your client. Get the notes.

    Mia records with consent, transcribes and summarises every meeting, then files it to the right client.

    • Calendar & video syncMeetings from your calendar and video calls are picked up automatically.
    • Speaker-labelled transcriptEvery word, with who said it, for video calls and in-person meetings.
    • Summary, insights and next stepsA clean summary is ready before you leave the room.
    • Filed to the right clientNotes and client emails land on the client’s profile, no copying across.

  2. A financial profile that builds itself.

    What the client earns, owns and owes is pulled from the conversation into one clear profile.

    • Cash flow, assets and debtsFilled in from what the client already told you.
    • Adjust in a tapCorrect any number before it goes into a plan.
    • Straight into goalsThe profile feeds the FNA, so nothing is typed twice.
    • Documents on the profileIDs, policies and statements kept with the client, in one place.

  3. FNAs in minutes, not evenings.

    Start a financial needs analysis from the profile and track every one across your book.

    • Every FNA in one listCompleted, drafts and those waiting on a signature.
    • Needs calculated for youInsurance, retirement and education gaps worked out.
    • E-signature built inSend for signature and see when it comes back.

  4. Walk in prepared.

    Before every meeting Mia writes a brief: who the client is, what changed and what to talk about.

    • Client snapshotPolicies, household and history on one page.
    • Client timelineEvery meeting, email and form with this client, in order.
    • Talking pointsOpen items and life changes worth raising.
    • Join from the briefOpen the client or join the call in one click.

  5. Know your compliance health.

    See which clients have their FNA, consent and disclosure on file, and fix gaps in one go.

    • Book health at a glanceOne number for how complete your files are.
    • Gaps by clientSee exactly what is missing for each person.
    • Send reminders in bulkAsk every client for what’s missing at once.

  6. Nothing slips through.

    Advisor watches renewal dates for you and puts the next ones on a timeline before they’re due.

    • Renewals on a timelineEverything due in the next 60 days, in order.
    • Reminders before it’s lateA nudge at 30 days and again near the date.
    • A daily briefWhat needs you today, every morning.
