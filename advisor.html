Everything Advisor does

Six features. One AI Workflow.

Each one removes a job you used to do by hand.

  1. 01 · AI meeting notes

    Talk to your client. Get the notes.

    MIA records (with consent), transcribes and summarises every meeting — then files it to the right client.

    • Speaker-labelled transcript
    • Summary, key insights and next steps
    • Works for video calls and in-person meetings

    30–45 min of notes after every meetingA 2-min review

  2. 02 · 1-click KYC & FNA

    Forms that fill themselves.

    Everything the client said becomes the KYC and FNA — pre-filled, checked for gaps, and sent for e-signature.

    • Pre-filled from the conversation
    • Missing fields highlighted
    • E-signature built in

    Re-typing answers into formsReview and send

  3. 03 · Suitability engine

    Every recommendation, justified.

    Each product you recommend is checked against the client’s profile, and the reasoning is written down for you.

    • Checks against goals, budget and risk
    • Rationale drafted in plain language
    • Flags when a replacement needs more care

    Writing justifications from memoryApprove the draft

  4. 04 · Chat with MIA

    Ask, don’t search.

    Ask anything about any client, policy or deadline. MIA answers with the source — and offers to do the next step.

    • Answers cite the meeting or document
    • Turns answers into actions
    • Works across all your clients

    Digging through foldersOne question

  5. 05 · E&O trail

    Your evidence, always on.

    Every meeting, form, check and approval is time-stamped and kept — exportable as one file when you need it.

    • Unedited, time-stamped log
    • One-click export per client
    • Visible to your firm in Harmony

    Hoping the paperwork is somewhereProof in a click

  6. 06 · Reminders & renewals

    Nothing slips through.

    Advisor watches dates for you — KYC refreshes, renewals, expiring documents — and prepares the work before it’s due.

    • Renewal and review reminders
    • KYC refresh prepared in advance
    • Daily brief of what needs you
