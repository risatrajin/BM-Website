Design system

Colours, type, spacing, motion and every component on the site, rendered with the site's own CSS. Change a variable in styles.css or sections.css and this page and the site change together.

Colour

Values and contrast ratios are measured live from the stylesheets. Green ratios pass WCAG AA for body text (4.5:1).

Mia Mascot

Mia's face. The eyes follow the cursor and switch shape when it moves past either side: a notch toward the left, plain ovals in the middle, a notch toward the right. It blinks every few seconds and glances around on its own when the pointer is idle or on touch.

Live · move the cursor around the page
Looking left · assets/mia/mascot-left.svg
Centre · assets/mia/mascot-center.svg
Looking right · assets/mia/mascot-right.svg

Usage: inline the SVG and add data-mascot to its wrapper, and ds.js wires the tracking. The body fills with currentColor, set to --ink-black by default. The eyes are always white. Under reduced motion the eyes still track the cursor, but there is no blinking or idle glancing.

Buttons & links

One class, .btn, plus a size and a variant. Every button is a pill with the same colours — ink, ink tints, and the accent only as the primary's hover fill. On dark surfaces wrap them in .on-dark and the same variants flip to white. sections.js adds the flow parts (arrows + fill circle); just write the classes.

<a class="btn btn--primary btn--lg" href="#">Start free</a>
<a class="btn btn--border btn--md" href="#">All resources</a>
<div class="on-dark"><a class="btn btn--secondary btn--lg">Book a demo</a></div>
